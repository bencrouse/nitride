## Original r1 playing guide

Use detached notes and hold through the swell. The envelope times are in seconds,
not tempo-synced. Start the key **before** the beat on which you want the terminal
attack to land. Very short taps abort the swell; this is intentional, not a delayed
sample that continues after key-up. Holding longer lets each gesture finish once;
it does not repeat or settle into a sustained pad.

| Study | Logic category | Swell | Intended identity / playing cue |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| **Backspun Tines** | Keys & Organs | 0.90 s | Rounded electric-key body gathers a late hammer/tine edge. Isolated keys and sparse minor chords. |
| **Unstruck Bell** | Plucks & Mallets | 1.60 s | Inharmonic bell ring pulls toward its metallic endpoint. Single notes or open dyads; allow the whole swell. |
| **Spool Guitar** | Plucks & Mallets | 0.48 s | Woody plucked-string surrogate: body before pick. Detached mid/low dyads and short figures. |
| **Inward Tom** | Drums & Percussion | 0.36 s | Low drum resonance grows into a compact skin-like edge. Try MIDI 36–55; hold beyond the swell to hear it end automatically. |
| **Suction Bass** | Bass | 0.42 s | Rounded root pulls into a resonant finger-like bite, then disappears. Detached low roots, no glide or wobble. |
| **Rewind Reed** | Leads & Brass | 0.68 s | Hollow wind-like body opens into a late reed bite. Detached mid-register phrases, not legato. |
| **Folded Strings** | Pads | 1.85 s | Longer ensemble-chord gesture ends at a bow-like edge instead of sustaining. Hold a chord together; leave space after it. |
| **Vanishing Clav** | Keys & Organs | 0.20 s | Quick dry backwards electric-key flick for repeated rhythmic notes. Hold at least a quarter-second. |

These describe design intentions, not a listening verdict or an acoustic-model claim.
The set deliberately spans short rhythmic gestures through longer ringing chords;
the user will judge whether they convincingly suggest familiar sounds in reverse.

## Original r1 envelope mechanism and boundaries

- Amplitude attacks range from 0.20–1.85 seconds. Sustain is zero, with a 25 ms
  exponential decay constant. The result falls quickly after its peak, but is not
  an instantaneous gate or an exact time-reversed exponential decay.
- FM brightness grows independently toward the endpoint; the shared Tone envelope
  opens alongside it. FM ratio/shape/mix and register preserve different instrument
  roles rather than using one generic filtered swell for every patch.
- Amplitude and FM envelopes are per voice. Tone is shared: staggered chord entries
  can reshape the whole chord. Start chord tones together for the clearest gesture.
- All LFOs, glide, legato and Autobend are disabled. There is no transport dependency,
  pre-trigger lookahead, MIDI generation or note-reset modulation workaround.
- Almost everything is dry; Unstruck Bell has only a small ordinary Space return.
  That return is not reversed and can leave a small tail after the instrument ends.
- There is no reversal of oscillator phase, noise, room impulse or recorded transients.
  Key duration controls whether the envelope reaches its endpoint; the synth cannot
  predict a future note-off or automatically place its peak on an arbitrary beat.
