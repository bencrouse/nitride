## Listen without confusing routing, drive or loudness

Private full-performance comparisons are in
`evaluation/filters/factory-001/listen/`. Every file plays:

1. The current **Classic** factory parent.
2. The same preset in **clean Rage** (amount zero).
3. The proposed **character and amount**.

The original MIDI is complete and identical in all three sections, with half-second
gaps. All three have one shared active-RMS target and at least 3 dB peak headroom;
this is not LUFS matching. Original unnormalized audio and timing/level metrics are
retained separately. B versus C isolates nonlinear character; A versus B exposes
the move from shared post-mix Tone to per-voice pre-amplitude filtering. Phase/response
differences in numerical residuals are not listening approval or pure alias energy.

Start with **Rubber Relay, Reed Snap, Bent Aerial, Brown Console, Taffy Coil and
Shred Relay**. Then check the remaining candidates. Use soft/hard playing and
staggered chords as well as the original MIDI. Listen for the preset's original job,
not merely whether the effect is audible. Taffy Coil and Low Battery Piano have
smaller nonlinear residuals than the more demonstrative bass/sequence examples;
their articulation and restraint matter more than forcing every example to distort
equally. Reject a candidate if its original contrast disappears.
